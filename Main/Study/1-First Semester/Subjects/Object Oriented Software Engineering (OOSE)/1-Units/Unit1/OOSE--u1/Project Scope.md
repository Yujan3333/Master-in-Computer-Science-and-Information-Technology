**Project Scope** defines the **boundaries, goals, deliverables, features, tasks, and limits** of a project. It answers **what will be done** and **what will not be done**.

---

### 🔑 Key Points:

- **Defines the work** to be completed in the project.
    
- Helps avoid **scope creep** (adding extra features later without approval).
    
- Includes **objectives, timelines, budget, and major functionalities**.
    
- Clearly states **what is included** and **excluded** from the project.
    

---

### 🛠 Example (Library Management System):

**In-scope:**

- Book issuing and returning
    
- Member registration
    
- Fine calculation
    

**Out-of-scope:**

- Online book purchase
    
- Integration with e-commerce platforms
    

---

### ✅ Why Project Scope is Important:

- Ensures everyone has a **common understanding** of the project.
    
- Helps in **planning, budgeting, and resource allocation**.
    
- Prevents **misunderstanding** and **uncontrolled changes**.
