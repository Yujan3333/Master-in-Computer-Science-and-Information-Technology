**“Data mining system intrudes the privacy of individuals”**

Data mining can violate privacy because it collects, analyzes, and combines personal data, often without the user’s full awareness.

Important points (write any 4–5):

* Data mining gathers **large amounts of personal data** (age, location, habits, purchases, searches).
* Combining different databases can **reveal sensitive information** about a person.
* Users may be **tracked without consent** through cookies, logs, and online activities.
* Can lead to **profiling** (behavior, interests, health, finance, beliefs).
* Risk of **data misuse, leaks, or hacking**.
* May cause **discrimination** (insurance, jobs, loans).
* Individuals lose **control over their personal information**.

Example:

* Online shopping sites predicting personal interests.
* Social media tracking behavior for targeted ads.

Conclusion line (for exam):

> Although data mining is useful, improper collection and use of personal data can seriously intrude on individual privacy, so strong security and privacy laws are necessary.
