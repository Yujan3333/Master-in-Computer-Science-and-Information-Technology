### ✅ 1. Prevent Malicious Misuse

- Protect the system from **intentional attacks** by users or programs.
    
- Stop unauthorized access to files, memory, or hardware.
    

---

### ✅ 2. Enforce Proper Use of Shared Resources

- Ensure that **shared resources** (e.g., memory, CPU, files, printers) are used **only according to system policies**.
    
- Prevent one user from disrupting others or hogging resources.
    

---

### ✅ 3. Minimize Damage by Faulty Programs

- If a program **crashes or misbehaves**, the system should **contain the damage**.
    
- Prevent it from affecting **other programs** or the **system as a whole**.
