#RGIS #assignment 

# Given

* Flying height above sea level $(H)=10\ \text{km}=10,000\ \text{m}$
* Focal length $(f)=150\ \text{mm}$
* Ground elevations:

  * Point A: $h_1=1200\ \text{m}$
  * Point B: $h_2=1960\ \text{m}$
* Average ground elevation:

  * $h_{avg}=1500\ \text{m}$

---

# Formula for Photo Scale

For a vertical aerial photograph:

$$S=\frac{f}{H-h}$$

Where:

* $S$ = photo scale
* $f$ = focal length
* $H$ = flying height above sea level
* $h$ = ground elevation

Convert all quantities into the same unit (mm).

---

# Convert Heights into Millimeters

$$H=10,000\ \text{m}=10,000,000\ \text{mm}$$

$$h_1=1200\ \text{m}=1,200,000\ \text{mm}$$

$$h_2=1960\ \text{m}=1,960,000\ \text{mm}$$

$$h_{avg}=1500\ \text{m}=1,500,000\ \text{mm}$$

---

# 1. Photo Scale at Elevation 1200 m

## Height above Ground

$$H-h_1=10,000,000-1,200,000$$

$$=8,800,000\ \text{mm}$$

## Scale

$$S_1=\frac{150}{8,800,000}$$

$$=\frac{1}{58,666.67}$$

---

# Answer

$$\boxed{S_1\approx1:58,667}$$

---

# 2. Photo Scale at Elevation 1960 m

## Height above Ground

$$H-h_2=10,000,000-1,960,000$$

$$=8,040,000\ \text{mm}$$

## Scale

$$S_2=\frac{150}{8,040,000}$$

$$=\frac{1}{53,600}$$

---

# Answer

$$\boxed{S_2\approx1:53,600}$$

---

# 3. Nominal Scale Using Average Elevation 1500 m

## Height above Average Ground

$$H-h_{avg}=10,000,000-1,500,000$$

$$=8,500,000\ \text{mm}$$

## Nominal Scale

$$S_n=\frac{150}{8,500,000}$$

$$=\frac{1}{56,666.67}$$

---

# Final Answer

| Case                                      | Photo Scale  |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------ |
| At elevation 1200 m                       | $$1:58,667$$ |
| At elevation 1960 m                       | $$1:53,600$$ |
| Nominal scale at average elevation 1500 m | $$1:56,667$$ |
