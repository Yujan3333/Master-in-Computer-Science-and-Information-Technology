## 🎞️ Video Compression Standards: MPEG
- **MPEG** stands for **Motion Picture Experts Group** — an international working group that develops **standards** for compressing **audio and video**.

> MPEG standards are designed for **efficient storage and streaming** of multimedia data.

- 📷 **Based on JPEG:**
	- MPEG video compression uses techniques from **JPEG** (Joint Photographic Experts Group), which is used for **compressing images**.


---
### 📦 MPEG Compression: Multi-layered Format

|**Layer**|**Content**|
|---|---|
|**Layer 1**|System and **timing** information (sync)|
|**Layer 2**|**Video stream**|
|**Layer 3**|**Audio and text** streams|

---


## 📘 Major MPEG Standards:

---

### 🎬 **1. MPEG-1**

- 📏 **Resolution**: 352 × 240 pixels
    
- 📶 **Bitrate**: < 1.5 Mbps
    
- 📼 **Quality**: Lower than VHS
    
- 🔊 **Audio**: Layer 3 → gave rise to **MP3 format**
    

**💡 Used for:**

- Video clips on the internet
    
- CD-ROM video
    
- Older portable devices
    

---

### 📺 **2. MPEG-2**

- 📏 **Resolution**: Standard TV & **HDTV**
    
- 📶 **Bitrate**: 1.5 – 15 Mbps
    
- 📀 **Used for**: **DVD** video encoding, digital TV
    

**💡 Used in:**

- **DVD players**
    
- **Cable and satellite broadcasts**
    
- **Blu-ray** (with enhancements)
    

---

### 🎮 **3. MPEG-4**

- Combines **audio**, **video**, **text**, and **interactive graphics**
    
- Supports **2D and 3D animations**
    
- More advanced **compression** than MPEG-2
    
- **Object-oriented**, allows interaction with multimedia content
    

**💡 Used in:**

- **Streaming platforms** (YouTube, Netflix)
    
- **Mobile video** and **video conferencing**
    
- Formats like **MP4** (video container)
    

---

## ✅ Summary Table

|MPEG Version|Features & Uses|
|---|---|
|**MPEG-1**|Low-res video, < 1.5 Mbps, MP3 audio, internet clips|
|**MPEG-2**|DVD, digital TV, HDTV; 1.5–15 Mbps|
|**MPEG-4**|Web, mobile, interactive media, 2D/3D graphics|
