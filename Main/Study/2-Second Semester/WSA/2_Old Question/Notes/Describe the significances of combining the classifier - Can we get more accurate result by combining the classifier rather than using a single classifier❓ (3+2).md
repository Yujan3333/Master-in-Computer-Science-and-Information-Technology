### **Significance of Combining Classifiers (3 marks)**

Combining classifiers (also called **ensemble learning**) means using **multiple models together** to make a final prediction instead of relying on a single classifier.

**Key significances:**

1. **Improved Accuracy**
   Different classifiers capture different patterns in data. Combining them reduces individual model errors and usually leads to better overall accuracy.

2. **Reduced Overfitting**
   A single classifier may overfit the training data. Combining multiple classifiers averages out errors, improving generalization.

3. **Increased Robustness and Stability**
   If one classifier performs poorly on certain instances, others can compensate, making the system more reliable.

---

### **Accuracy Comparison: Combined vs Single Classifier (2 marks)**

**Yes, combining classifiers can produce more accurate results than using a single classifier**, provided:

* The classifiers are **diverse**
* Errors made by classifiers are **not highly correlated**
* Proper combination methods are used

Examples of combination methods:

* **Bagging**
* **Boosting**
* **Random Forest**
* **Voting ensembles**

---

### **Conclusion (Exam-ready line)**

> Combining classifiers improves accuracy, reduces overfitting, and increases robustness, and therefore often yields better performance than a single classifier.
