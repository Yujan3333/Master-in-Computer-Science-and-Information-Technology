- Why is early morning often preferred for aerial remote sensing missions? *(2082, Q7 — partial)*
- What are the functions of filters in remote sensing camera systems? An image shows a measured road length of 4 cm; ground length = 1.2 km. Calculate the image scale. *(2082, Q8 — 5 marks, numerical)*
- Why is geometric correction of an image useful prior to further analysis and interpretation? Explain. Numerical: Aerial photo at height 3000 m AMSL, focal length 150 mm, average ground elevation 450 m AMSL — calculate scale of photograph. *(2081, Q8 — 5 marks, numerical)*
- Describe advantages and disadvantages of vertical and oblique aerial photographs. *(2071, Q5)*
- What are the detectors of film's exposure? Describe it briefly. *(2071, Q6)*

**Most Important Topic:** **Scale of photograph calculation and Relief Displacement numericals** — a numerical from this chapter appears in almost every exam (Pre-board, 2082, 2081). Master the scale formula (f/H) and relief displacement formula.

## Chapter 9: Digital Image Processing

**Old Questions:**
- Discuss different image preprocessing techniques and explain the advantages of geometric correction before image analysis. *(Midterm 2026, Q2 — 5 marks)*
- Explain why filtering is applied in pre-processing even before enhancement or transformation. Differentiate between supervised and unsupervised classification techniques with suitable examples. *(2082, Q1 — 10 marks)*
- What is Support Vector Machine (SVM)? What are hard margin and soft margin in SVM? Discuss about a linear classifier. *(2078, Q3 — 10 marks)*
- Explain about bagging. What are out-of-bag (OOB) samples? *(2078, Q4 — 5 marks)*

- Explain following terminologies: Top of atmosphere reflectance, Surface reflectance. *(2078, Q8 — 5 marks)*
- Landsat 8 OLI has 10 multi-spectral bands of 30m spatial resolution having 6000 lines and 6600 samples, and one panchromatic band of 15m spatial resolution with 16-bit pixel size. Calculate the size of image. *(2076, Q1 — 10 marks, numerical)*
- Explain image encoding and its types with example. *(2076, Q2 — 5 marks)*
- Write about k-means algorithm. Implement the K-means clustering for the given dataset with k=2. *(2076, Q3 — 10 marks, numerical/practical)*
- Explain about low pass kernel with example. *(2076, Q4 — 5 marks)*
- Differentiate between supervised and unsupervised classification. *(2076, Q5 — 5 marks)*
- Explain following terminologies: Digital number (DN), Surface reflectance. *(2076, Q8 — 5 marks)*

**Most Important Topic:** This is the **largest and most numerically-heavy chapter**. Key recurring items:

3. K-means / ISODATA clustering
4. Preprocessing & Geometric Correction
5. Terminologies: DN, TOA reflectance, Surface reflectance (asked as a fixed 5-mark question almost every year)
