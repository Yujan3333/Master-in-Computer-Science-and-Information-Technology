#rgis #third-semester 

| Feature                  | Active Sensor                                                          | Passive Sensor                                                         |
| ------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Definition**           | Emits its own electromagnetic energy and measures the reflected signal | Detects natural electromagnetic energy reflected or emitted by objects |
| **Energy Source**        | Sensor itself                                                          | Sun (reflected energy) or Earth's natural thermal emission             |
| **Works at Night?**      | Yes                                                                    | Usually no (except thermal infrared sensors)                           |
| **Depends on Sunlight?** | No                                                                     | Yes (for visible and near-infrared imaging)                            |
| **Weather Effect**       | Radar can operate through clouds, rain, and fog                        | Clouds and haze can block or reduce data quality                       |
| **Data Collected**       | Backscattered energy                                                   | Reflected or emitted natural radiation                                 |
| **Cost**                 | More expensive                                                         | Less expensive                                                         |
| **Examples**             | RADAR, SAR, LiDAR                                                      | Cameras, Multispectral sensors, Landsat, Sentinel-2                    |
