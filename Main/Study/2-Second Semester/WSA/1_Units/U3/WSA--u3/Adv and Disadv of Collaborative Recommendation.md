## **Advantages of Collaborative Filtering (5 points)**

1. **No need for item details**
   – Works only with user ratings, not item descriptions.

2. **Personalized recommendations**
   – Different users get different recommendations.

3. **Learns automatically**
   – Improves as more users give ratings.

4. **Domain independent**
   – Can be used for movies, songs, products, books, etc.

5. **Finds hidden patterns**
   – Can recommend items you might not search for yourself.

---

## **Disadvantages of Collaborative Filtering (5 points)**

1. **Cold start problem**
   – New users or new items have no ratings.

2. **Sparsity problem**
   – Most users rate very few items, making prediction hard.

3. **Scalability issues**
   – Computation becomes slow with many users and items.

4. **Popularity bias**
   – Popular items are recommended more than niche items.

5. **Vulnerability to fake ratings**
   – Can be manipulated by malicious users.

---

### **Super-short memory trick 🧠**

* **Pros:** No content, personal, learns, flexible, smart
* **Cons:** Cold start, sparse data, slow at scale, bias, spam
