### ✅ **Non-Uniform Data Rate**

- Video data isn’t sent at a constant rate.
    
- Some frames or scenes need **more bits** (more detail), others less.
    
- This is called **variable bit rate** — saves space without hurting quality.
    

---

### ✅ **Compression Ratios**

- High compression ratios like **50:1 to 80:1** are possible.
    
- This means the compressed video is 50 to 80 times smaller than original.
    

---

### ✅ **Asymmetric Algorithm**

- Encoding (compressing) is **slow and complex** — involves searching and analyzing images to find differences.
    
- Decoding (playing back) is **fast and simple** — like JPEG decoding.
    

---

### ✅ **Hardware Support**

- Many cheap and fast decoding chips exist.
    
- Used in:
    
    - Graphics cards
        
    - DVD players
        
    - Set-top boxes
        

---

### 🧠 **Summary:**

> Video compression uses **complex encoding** but **simple, fast decoding** with **variable data rates** and **high compression ratios** for efficient storage and playback.
