# ✅ **1. Steps in Remote Sensing (Remote Sensing Process)**

Remote sensing involves a sequence of steps through which information about the Earth is collected and analyzed.

---

## 🔹 **Steps of Remote Sensing**

### **1. Source of Energy**

Energy is required for remote sensing, usually from the **Sun (natural source)** or from an artificial source (in active sensing).

---

### **2. Interaction with Atmosphere**

The energy travels through the atmosphere where it may be **scattered or absorbed** before reaching the Earth’s surface.

---

### **3. Interaction with Target (Earth Surface)**

The energy interacts with objects on the Earth (land, water, vegetation) and is **reflected, absorbed, or transmitted**.

---

### **4. Recording by Sensor**

Sensors (on satellites or aircraft) detect and record the reflected or emitted energy.

---

### **5. Transmission, Reception, and Processing**

The recorded data is transmitted to ground stations, where it is processed into usable images.

---

### **6. Interpretation and Analysis**

The processed images are interpreted visually or digitally to extract useful information.

---

### **7. Application**

The final information is used in various fields such as:

* Agriculture
* Disaster management
* Urban planning
* Environmental monitoring

---

# ✅ **2. Active and Passive Sensors**

Remote sensing systems are classified based on the source of energy.

---

## 🔹 **Passive Sensors**

### 🔸 **Definition**

Passive sensors **use natural energy**, mainly sunlight, reflected from the Earth.

---

### 🔸 **Characteristics**

* Depend on sunlight
* Cannot work at night (except thermal)
* Affected by clouds and atmosphere

---

### 🔸 **Examples**

* Optical satellite sensors
* Cameras

---

---

## 🔹 **Active Sensors**

### 🔸 **Definition**

Active sensors **generate their own energy** and measure the reflected signal.

---

### 🔸 **Characteristics**

* Work day and night
* Not affected by cloud cover (in many cases, e.g., radar)
* Provide their own illumination

---

### 🔸 **Examples**

* Radar
* LiDAR

---

# 🔍 **Difference Between Active and Passive Sensors**

| **Aspect**         | **Passive Sensor** | **Active Sensor**           |
| ------------------ | ------------------ | --------------------------- |
| **Energy Source**  | Natural (Sun)      | Artificial (self-generated) |
| **Working Time**   | Daytime only       | Day and night               |
| **Weather Effect** | Affected by clouds | Less affected               |
| **Signal**         | Reflected sunlight | Reflected emitted signal    |
| **Examples**       | Optical sensors    | Radar, LiDAR                |

---

# ✅ **Conclusion**

Remote sensing follows a systematic process from energy source to data analysis. Passive sensors depend on natural energy, while active sensors use their own energy, making them more flexible in different conditions.
