## Latitude and Longitude

Latitude and longitude are **geographic coordinates** used to locate any place on the Earth's surface.

---

## 1. Latitude (Horizontal Lines)

**Definition:**
Latitude is the angular distance of a place **north or south of the Equator**, measured in degrees (°).

* Measured from **0° to 90°**
* Lines run **east-west** (horizontal), but measure **north-south** position.

### Important Latitudes

```text
90° N   → North Pole
66.5° N → Arctic Circle
23.5° N → Tropic of Cancer
0°      → Equator
23.5° S → Tropic of Capricorn
66.5° S → Antarctic Circle
90° S   → South Pole
```

### Example

* Kathmandu: **27.7172° N**
* New Delhi: **28.6139° N**
* Sydney: **33.8688° S**

---

## 2. Longitude (Vertical Lines)

**Definition:**
Longitude is the angular distance of a place **east or west of the Prime Meridian**, measured in degrees (°).

* Measured from **0° to 180°**
* Lines run **north-south** (vertical), but measure **east-west** position.

### Important Longitude

```text
180° W ←----------- 0° -----------→ 180° E
                   Prime Meridian
                   (Greenwich)
```

### Example

* Kathmandu: **85.3240° E**
* London: **0°**
* New York: **74.0060° W**

## Difference Between Latitude and Longitude

| Latitude                         | Longitude                           |
| -------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Measures north–south position    | Measures east–west position         |
| Starts from the Equator (0°)     | Starts from the Prime Meridian (0°) |
| Range: 0°–90° N/S                | Range: 0°–180° E/W                  |
| Lines are horizontal (east-west) | Lines are vertical (north-south)    |
| Parallel lines; never meet       | Meridians; meet at the poles        |
| Used to determine climate zones  | Used to determine time zones        |

---

## Easy Trick to Remember

* **Latitude = Ladder** → Imagine climbing a ladder **up and down** from the Equator toward the poles (north/south).
* **Longitude = Long way around** → Measures how far **east or west** you are around the Earth.
