## 📊 **Key QoS Metrics**

---

### 1. **Throughput**

- Total amount of work or data **completed or processed** in a given time.
    
- Measured in bits per second (bps) or tasks per second.
    
- Higher throughput means the system can handle more data quickly.
    

---

### 2. **Delay (Latency)**

- Time elapsed from when a request is made until the result is received.
    
- Lower delay means faster response.
    

---

### 3. **Jitter**

- Variability in delay during the playback of a stream (like video or audio).
    
- Causes problems like choppy video or audio.
    
- Lower jitter means smoother playback.
    

---

### 4. **Reliability**

- How well errors during transmission and processing are handled.
    
- Ensures continuous media (audio/video) is delivered without glitches or loss.
    

---

## 🧠 **Summary**

|Metric|Meaning|Why Important|
|---|---|---|
|**Throughput**|Amount of data done per time|Handles workload efficiently|
|**Delay**|Time from request to result|Faster user experience|
|**Jitter**|Variation in delay during playback|Smooth video/audio|
|**Reliability**|Handling errors in transmission|Continuous media quality|
