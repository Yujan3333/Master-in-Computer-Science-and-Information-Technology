
## 🖥️ Multimedia System Architectures – Simple to Modern

---

### ✅ 1. Simple Architecture:

- In early multimedia systems, **audio and video data paths** were **separate** from the CPU's main computational data path.
    
- Specialized **I/O paths** or devices handled audio/video independently.
    

📌 **Key Points:**

- Less load on CPU
    
- Limited by slower components and bus speeds
    
- Example: Older CD/DVD-based video players or analog capture cards
    

---

### ✅ 2. Modern Architecture:

Modern systems use **high-speed buses**, **powerful CPUs**, and **integrated multimedia hardware**.

📌 **Key Components:**

|Component|Role|
|---|---|
|**Fast System Bus**|Transfers data quickly between memory, CPU, and devices|
|**CPU/GPU**|Handles audio/video processing and compression/decompression|
|**Multimedia Devices**|High-performance sound cards, graphic cards, webcams|

Examples:

- Smart TVs
    
- Smartphones
    
- Video editing workstations
    

---

### ✅ 3. Video Server Architecture:

Used in systems that handle **multiple simultaneous video streams**, like:

- **Streaming services (e.g., YouTube, Netflix)**
    
- **Video conferencing platforms**
    

📌 **Main Features:**

|Feature|Description|
|---|---|
|**Disk Farm**|Large array of disks for storing high-volume video data|
|**Multiple Streams**|Support many users/viewers at once without delay|
|**Load Balancing**|Distributes video data requests across multiple servers|

---

## 🧠 Summary:

|Type|Description|
|---|---|
|**Simple**|Separate A/V paths from computation paths|
|**Modern**|Integrated multimedia processing with fast buses|
|**Video Server**|Disk farms + stream management for large-scale services|
