###  Apply at least two image enhancement techniques (e.g., contrast stretching, histogram equalization). 

#### Image Enhancement techniques
Go to:

```text
Raster Properties → Symbology
```

Select:

```text
Min/Max Value Settings
→ Stretch to MinMax
```

Apply.
![](../../../../../Images/Third_Sem_Images/Lab%20Done%20By%20Me-Lab4-1.png)

#### Histogram equalization
`Stretch and Clip to MinMax`

`Load Min/Max Values`
Choose:
**Cumulative Count Cut**
Example:
`2% - 98%`
