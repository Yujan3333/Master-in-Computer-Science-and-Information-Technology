# Cryptography

## Definition

**Cryptography** is the science and art of securing information by converting plaintext into ciphertext using cryptographic algorithms and keys.

It focuses on designing secure techniques such as:

* Encryption
* Decryption
* Hash functions
* Digital signatures
* Message Authentication Codes (MACs)

---

# Cryptosystem

## Definition

A **cryptosystem** is a complete system that uses cryptographic algorithms and keys to perform encryption and decryption.

A cryptosystem consists of:

* Plaintext
* Encryption algorithm
* Key
* Ciphertext
* Decryption algorithm

# Easy Analogy

Think of **medicine** and a **hospital**.

* **Cryptography** = Medical science (the knowledge).
* **Cryptosystem** = A hospital using that knowledge to treat patients.

Similarly:

* **Cryptography** = The theory and techniques.
* **Cryptosystem** = The actual system that uses those techniques.

# Exam Answer (2 Marks)

> **Cryptography** is the science of securing information using encryption and related techniques. A **cryptosystem** is the practical implementation of cryptography that consists of plaintext, ciphertext, encryption and decryption algorithms, and keys. Thus, cryptography is the theory, whereas a cryptosystem is the complete system that applies that theory.
