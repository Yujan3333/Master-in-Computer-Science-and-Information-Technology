---

| **Term**  | **Stands for**          | **What it is**                                                                            | **Example**                                                      |
| --------- | ----------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 🤖 **AI** | Artificial Intelligence | Any technique that enables machines to **mimic human intelligence**                       | A robot playing chess, a chatbot, smart assistants (Siri, Alexa) |
| 📊 **ML** | Machine Learning        | A **subset of AI** where machines learn from **data** without being explicitly programmed | Spam email detection, product recommendations                    |
| 🧠 **DL** | Deep Learning           | A **subset of ML** that uses **neural networks with many layers** to learn complex things | Self-driving cars, facial recognition, language translation      |
