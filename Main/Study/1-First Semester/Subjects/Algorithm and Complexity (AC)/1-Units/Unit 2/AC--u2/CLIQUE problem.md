### ✅ **What is a Clique?**

- A **clique** in a graph is a subset of vertices such that **every pair of vertices is connected by an edge**.
    
- In other words, it's a **complete subgraph**.
    

---

### ✅ **Maximum Clique Problem (Definition):**

> Find the **largest possible clique** in a given graph.  
> That is, find the **maximum number of vertices** that form a complete subgraph.
