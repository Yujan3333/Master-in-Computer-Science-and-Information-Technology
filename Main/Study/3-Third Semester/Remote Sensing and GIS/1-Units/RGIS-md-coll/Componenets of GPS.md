# Components (Segments) of GPS

GPS consists of **three segments**:

1. **Space Segment (Satellites)**
2. **Control Segment (Ground Stations)**
3. **User Segment (GPS Receivers)**

---

# 1. Space Segment (GPS Satellites)

## Definition

The **Space Segment** consists of GPS satellites orbiting the Earth. These satellites continuously transmit their **position and time** to GPS receivers.

### Main Points

* Consists of about **31–32 GPS satellites**.
* Orbit at **20,200 km** above the Earth.
* Arranged in **6 orbital planes**.
* Complete one orbit in about **12 hours** (11 h 58 min).
* At least **4 satellites** are visible from any location on Earth.
* Powered by **solar panels**.
* Each satellite has:

  * Atomic clocks
  * Computer
  * Radio transmitter
* Continuously broadcasts:

  * Satellite position
  * Accurate time

### Functions

* Sends location and time signals.
* Provides worldwide GPS coverage.
* Enables accurate positioning.

---

# 2. Control Segment (Ground Control Stations)

## Definition

The **Control Segment** is a network of ground stations that **monitor, control, and update GPS satellites**.

### Main Parts

* Master Control Station (Colorado, USA)
* Monitor Stations around the world
* Ground antennas

### Functions

* Tracks satellite positions.
* Monitors satellite health.
* Corrects satellite clock errors.
* Updates satellite orbit information.
* Ensures satellites remain in their correct orbits.

### Simple Flow

```text
Ground Station
      │
Checks satellite
      │
Sends corrections
      ▼
GPS Satellite
```

---

# 3. User Segment (GPS Receivers)

## Definition

The **User Segment** consists of all **GPS receivers** used by people and organizations to determine their location.

### Examples

* Mobile phones
* Car navigation systems
* Survey GPS
* Aircraft GPS
* Ship GPS
* Handheld GPS

### Main Components of a Receiver

* Antenna
* Receiver processor
* Clock
* Display

### Functions

* Receives signals from satellites.
* Calculates:

  * Position (Latitude & Longitude)
  * Altitude
  * Speed
  * Time

# Easy Memory Trick

**S → C → U**

* **S = Space** → **Satellites send signals**
* **C = Control** → **Ground stations control satellites**
* **U = User** → **Receivers use the signals**

## Exam Definition (2 Marks)

**Space Segment:** Consists of GPS satellites that orbit the Earth and continuously transmit position and timing signals.

**Control Segment:** Consists of ground stations that monitor satellite health, correct orbital and clock errors, and upload updates to the satellites.

**User Segment:** Consists of GPS receivers (such as smartphones, vehicle navigation systems, and survey instruments) that receive satellite signals to determine location, speed, altitude, and time.
