
# 🟨 **Slide 1: Border / Marginal Information (Concept)**

### **What is it?**

* Information printed on the **edges (borders) of an image**
* Helps understand **how the data was captured and processed**

---

### **Why is it Important?**

* No local knowledge about data conditions
* Helps check:

  * Accuracy
  * Resolution
  * Scale
  * Time of capture
* Useful for **analysis and decision making**

---

### **Includes Information About**

* Satellite & sensor details
* Date and time
* Location (coordinates)
* Band combination
* Scale and resolution

# 🟦 **Slide 2: Types of Border Information**

### **1. Satellite Image Information**

* Satellite name and product type
* Sensor and band details
* Date & time of capture
* Coordinates and projection
* Processing details

---

### **2. Airphoto Information**

* Time and altitude
* Camera orientation (attitude)
* Flight line and photo number
* Unique ID for each image

---

### **Key Idea**

👉 *“Border information acts like metadata—it tells everything about the image needed for proper interpretation.”*

---

## 🎤 **What You Can Say (Simple)**

👉 *“Border or marginal information is the data written on image edges that tells us when, where, and how the image was captured, which helps in accurate analysis.”*
