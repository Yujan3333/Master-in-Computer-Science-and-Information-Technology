- Dunn  Index metric to evaluate the quality of clustering in machine learning
- Measures the Separation between clusters and the compactness within clusters
--- 
##### Formula
![](../../../../../../../Images/Second_Sem_Images/Dunn%20Index%20(DI)-formula.png)

**Inter Cluster Distance** - The Distance between the closest points of two different clusters
**Intra Cluster Distance** - The maximum distance between any two points within the same cluster
