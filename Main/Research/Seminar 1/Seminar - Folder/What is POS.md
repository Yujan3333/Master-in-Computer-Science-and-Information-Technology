### What is POS?

**POS** stands for **Part of Speech**. In natural language processing (NLP), **POS tagging** means labeling each word in a sentence with its grammatical category—like **noun**, **verb**, **adjective**, etc.

---

### Common POS Tags:

| Tag             | Meaning            | Example            |
| --------------- | ------------------ | ------------------ |
| **Noun**        | Person/place/thing | *food*, *service*  |
| **Verb**        | Action             | *eat*, *liked*     |
| **Adjective**   | Describes nouns    | *tasty*, *bad*     |
| **Adverb**      | Describes verbs    | *quickly*, *very*  |
| **Pronoun**     | Replaces noun      | *he*, *they*       |
| **Preposition** | Shows relation     | *in*, *at*, *with* |

---

### Why POS tagging is used:

In **sentiment analysis**, POS tagging helps identify which words are relevant to the **aspect** of interest. For example, it can help extract **nouns** (like *food*, *service*) as aspects and **adjectives** (like *delicious*, *poor*) as sentiment indicators.
