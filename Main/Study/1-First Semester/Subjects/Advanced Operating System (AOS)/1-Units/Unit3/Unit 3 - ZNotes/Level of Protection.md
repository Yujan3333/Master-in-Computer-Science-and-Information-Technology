Four Levels

### 🔐 1. Physical Level

#### 📌 Goal:

Prevent **unauthorized physical access** to system hardware.

#### 🧨 Common Threats:

- **Theft or tampering** of servers, computers, or storage devices
    
- **Hardware manipulation** (e.g., installing keyloggers or malicious USBs)
    

#### ✅ Protections:

- Locked server rooms, surveillance, ID cards, biometric access
    

---

### 👤 2. Human Level (Social Engineering)

#### 📌 Goal:

Protect users from **manipulation or trickery**.

#### 🧨 Common Attacks:

- **Social Engineering**: Tricking people into revealing confidential info
    
- **Phishing**: Fake emails or websites to steal login credentials
    
- **Dumpster Diving**: Searching trash for passwords, documents
    
- **Password Cracking**: Guessing or brute-forcing passwords
    

#### ✅ Protections:

- User training, strong password policies, two-factor authentication (2FA)
    

---

### 🖥️ 3. Operating System (OS) Level

#### 📌 Goal:

Secure the OS against **malware, privilege escalation, and unauthorized access**.

#### 🧨 Common Threats:

- Exploiting OS vulnerabilities
    
- Installing rootkits or trojans
    
- Misusing SUID/SGID privileges
    

#### ✅ Protections:

- Regular updates/patches
    
- RBAC (Role-Based Access Control)
    
- Access control mechanisms (ACLs, capabilities)
    

---

### 🌐 4. Network Level

#### 📌 Goal:

Protect data **in transit** and **prevent external intrusions**.

#### 🧨 Common Attacks:

- **Eavesdropping/sniffing** (passive attacks)
    
- **Man-in-the-middle** attacks
    
- **Denial of Service (DoS)**
    
- **Port scanning and intrusion**
    

#### ✅ Protections:

- Firewalls, Intrusion Detection/Prevention Systems (IDS/IPS)
    
- VPNs and encryption (SSL/TLS)
    
- Secure protocols (SSH, HTTPS)
    

---

## ✅ Summary Table

|Level|Common Attacks|Key Protection Methods|
|---|---|---|
|**Physical**|Theft, tampering|Locks, CCTV, access control systems|
|**Human**|Phishing, dumpster diving, password hacks|User training, 2FA, strong password policies|
|**OS**|Privilege escalation, malware|Updates, access control, antivirus|
|**Network**|DoS, MITM, eavesdropping|Firewalls, encryption, IDS, VPNs|
