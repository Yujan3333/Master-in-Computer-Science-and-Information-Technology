
## **BLOBs (Binary Large Objects)**

* **Definition:** BLOBs are **large chunks of binary data** stored in a database.

* **Purpose:** To store **complex or multimedia data** that cannot fit into normal columns.

* **Examples of BLOB data:**

  * Images
  * Audio files
  * Video files
  * Documents (PDFs, Word files)

* **Key Points in ORDBMS:**

  1. BLOBs may be **larger than a disk page**, requiring **special storage techniques**.
  2. Can be stored as part of **user-defined types (ADTs)**.
  3. Access may involve **pointers or chunking** to handle large sizes efficiently.

---

**Exam one-liner:**

> **BLOB = Binary Large Object = stores large multimedia or complex binary data in a database.**
