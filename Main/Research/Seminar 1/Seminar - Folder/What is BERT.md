### What is BERT?

**BERT** stands for **Bidirectional Encoder Representations from Transformers**. It’s a powerful **pretrained language model** developed by Google in 2018 that revolutionized natural language processing (NLP).

---

### Key Features:

* **Bidirectional**: Unlike older models that read text left-to-right or right-to-left, BERT reads **both directions simultaneously** to better understand context.
* **Transformer architecture**: It’s based on the Transformer model, which uses a mechanism called **self-attention** to weigh the importance of different words in a sentence.
* **Pretrained**: BERT is trained on a massive amount of text (like Wikipedia), so it learns language patterns before being fine-tuned for specific tasks like sentiment analysis, question answering, or text classification.
* **Fine-tuning**: You can adapt BERT to many NLP tasks with relatively little task-specific data.

---

### Why is BERT important?

Because of its deep understanding of language context, BERT has achieved **state-of-the-art performance** on many NLP benchmarks, outperforming previous models like LSTM, BiLSTM, and traditional machine learning approaches.

---

### Example use case

* Sentiment analysis: BERT can analyze the sentiment of a review by understanding subtle cues in the entire sentence or paragraph, improving accuracy.
* Named entity recognition, question answering, and more.
