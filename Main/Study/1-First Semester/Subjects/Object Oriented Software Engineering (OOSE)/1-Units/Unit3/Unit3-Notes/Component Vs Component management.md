### ✅ **1. Components**

**Definition**:  
A **component** is a **modular, reusable, and self-contained unit of software** that encapsulates implementation and exposes functionality through a well-defined interface.

#### 🔹 Characteristics of a Component:

- Has a **well-defined interface**.
    
- **Reusable** across different systems.
    
- Can be **independently developed** and deployed.
    
- **Encapsulates** functionality (e.g., login module, payment gateway, etc.).
    

#### ✅ Example:

In an online food delivery system:

- `User Authentication` can be one component.
    
- `Order Management` is another component.
    

---

### ✅ **2. Component Management**

**Definition**:  
**Component Management** refers to the **activities and processes involved in handling components** throughout the software development lifecycle.

#### 🔹 Tasks in Component Management:

- **Selecting** suitable components (existing or new).
    
- **Version control** of components.
    
- **Integration** of components into the system.
    
- **Updating**, maintaining, and replacing components.
    
- Ensuring **compatibility** between different components.
    

#### ✅ Example:

In your system, if you are using an open-source **Payment Gateway component**, managing its versions, updating it securely, and replacing it when deprecated is part of **component management**.
