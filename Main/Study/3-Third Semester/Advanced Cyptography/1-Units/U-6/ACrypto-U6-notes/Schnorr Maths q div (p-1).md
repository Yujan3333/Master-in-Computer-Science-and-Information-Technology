### What does

$$
q \mid (p-1)
$$

mean?

It means **(q) divides ((p-1)) exactly**, i.e., there is **no remainder**.

In simple words:

> **((p-1)) must be a multiple of (q).**

---

### Example

Suppose

$$
p = 23
$$

Then

$$
p-1 = 22
$$

Choose

$$
q = 11
$$

Now,

$$
22 \div 11 = 2
$$

Since there is **no remainder**,

$$
11 \mid 22
$$

✅ Condition satisfied.

---

If

$$
q = 5
$$

then

$$
22 \div 5 = 4.4
$$

There is a remainder, so

$$
5 \nmid 22
$$

❌ Condition not satisfied.

### **Memory Trick**

Whenever you see

$$
a \mid b
$$

read it as:

> **"a divides b"** or **"b is divisible by a."**
