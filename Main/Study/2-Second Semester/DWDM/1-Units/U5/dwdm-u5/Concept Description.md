## Concept Description

**Definition:**
Concept description is the task in data mining that **summarizes or describes the important characteristics of a dataset** in a concise and understandable way. It answers **“what is happening in the data?”** rather than predicting the future.

**Purpose:**

* To help understand the **main patterns or trends** in the data.
* To allow **comparison between classes** or groups.
* To simplify **large volumes of data** into meaningful summaries.

---

### Two Main Types of Concept Description

#### 1. Characterization

   * Summarizes a **single dataset**.
   * Example: “Most graduate students are aged 21–30 and major in Science.”

#### 2. Comparison

   * Compares **two or more datasets** to find differences.
   * Example: “Graduate students have higher GPA than undergraduate students in Science.”

---

### How It Works

* **Data Generalization:** Low-level detailed data is replaced with **higher-level concepts** (e.g., 22, 25, 28 → “21–30 years”).
* Methods used:

  1. **Data Cube / OLAP approach** – Uses aggregated summaries across dimensions.
  2. **Attribute-Oriented Induction (AOI)** – Uses **concept hierarchies** to generalize attribute values.

---

✅ **Key Point for Exam:**

> Concept description = **describing what the data says**
> Characterization = summary of one class
> Comparison = distinction between classes
