# GPS (Global Positioning System)

## Definition

**GPS (Global Positioning System)** is a **satellite-based navigation system** developed by the **United States** that provides accurate **position, navigation, and timing (PNT)** information anywhere on Earth.

### Exam Definition

> **GPS is a satellite-based navigation system that uses signals from satellites to determine a user's precise location (latitude, longitude, altitude), speed, and time.**

---

# How GPS Works

GPS works by receiving signals from multiple satellites.

```text
GPS Satellites
      │
      ▼
Radio Signals
      │
      ▼
GPS Receiver
      │
      ▼
Calculates Position
      │
      ▼
Latitude, Longitude, Altitude
```

A GPS receiver measures the travel time of signals from **at least 4 satellites** and uses **trilateration** to calculate its exact position.

---

# Components of GPS

GPS consists of **three segments**:

### 1. Space Segment

* A constellation of GPS satellites orbiting the Earth.
* Satellites continuously transmit position and time signals.

### 2. Control Segment

* Ground control stations monitor the satellites.
* They update satellite positions and correct clock errors.

### 3. User Segment

* GPS receivers used by people.
* Examples:

  * Smartphones
  * Car navigation systems
  * Survey equipment
  * Aircraft navigation systems

---

# Information Provided by GPS

GPS can determine:

* Latitude
* Longitude
* Altitude (Height)
* Speed
* Direction
* Accurate Time

---

# Applications of GPS

* Vehicle navigation
* Mobile phone navigation (Google Maps)
* Surveying and mapping
* Agriculture (precision farming)
* Disaster management
* Military operations
* Aviation
* Marine navigation
* Tracking vehicles and people

---

# Advantages of GPS

* High positioning accuracy
* Global coverage
* Available 24 hours a day
* Free for civilian use
* Fast and reliable navigation
* Integrates easily with GIS and remote sensing

---

# Limitations of GPS

* Requires a clear view of the sky
* Signals can be blocked by tall buildings, tunnels, or dense forests
* Accuracy can be affected by atmospheric conditions
* Multipath errors (signals reflecting off buildings)
* Depends on satellite availability

---

# GPS vs GNSS

| GPS                             | GNSS                                              |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Developed by the USA            | General term for all satellite navigation systems |
| One satellite navigation system | Includes GPS, GLONASS, Galileo, BeiDou, etc.      |
| Part of GNSS                    | Umbrella system                                   |

**Remember:** **GPS is a type of GNSS, but GNSS includes multiple global navigation systems.**
