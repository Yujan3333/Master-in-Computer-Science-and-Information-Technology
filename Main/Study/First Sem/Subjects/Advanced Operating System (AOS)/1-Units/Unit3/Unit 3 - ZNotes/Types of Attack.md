## 🛡️ **Types of Attacks in a Computer System**

Attacks are categorized based on **intent**, **method**, and **origin**. The major classifications are:

---

### 🔍 1. Passive vs Active Attacks

|Type of Attack|Description|
|---|---|

### 💤 **Passive Attack**

- **Goal:** To **gather information** without altering the system.
    
- The attacker remains **hidden and silent**.
    
- **No direct harm**, but **breaches confidentiality**.
    

📌 **Examples**:

- **Eavesdropping**: Listening to private communication.
    
- **Wiretapping**: Monitoring network or telephone lines.
    
- **Port Scanning**: Probing systems to find open ports/services.
    

---

### ⚔️ Active Attack

- **Goal:** To **modify**, **disrupt**, or **damage** system operations.
    
- The attacker **interferes** with normal functioning.
    
- Affects **integrity**, **availability**, or **authenticity**.
    

📌 **Examples**:

- **Denial of Service (DoS)**: Overloading a server to crash or slow it.
    
- **Man-in-the-Middle (MITM)**: Attacker intercepts and possibly alters communication between two parties.
    

---

### 🧑‍💻 2. Inside vs Outside Attacks

|Type of Attack|Description|
|---|---|

### 🏠 Inside Attack (Insider Threat)

- Initiated by someone **within the organization** (e.g., employee).
    
- Has **legitimate access**, misused for **malicious purposes**.
    

📌 Example:

- Disgruntled employee stealing or leaking sensitive data.
    

---

### 🌐 Outside Attack (External Threat)

- Comes from **outside the organization**, typically over the network.
    
- Involves **hackers, bots, or external attackers** trying to break into the system.
    

📌 Example:

- A hacker exploiting software vulnerabilities to gain system access.
    

---
