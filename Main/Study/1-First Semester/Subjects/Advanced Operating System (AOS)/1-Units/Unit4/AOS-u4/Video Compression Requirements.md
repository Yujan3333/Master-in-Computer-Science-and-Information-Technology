### ✅ **Key Requirements:**

---

### 1. 📉 High Compression Ratio

- Must achieve compression ratios **greater than 50:1**  
    (i.e., reduce file size by more than 50 times)
    

📌 **Example**:

- Original video size: **210 GB**
    
- Compressed to: **4.7 GB** (fits in a DVD)
    

---

### 2. 👁️ Visual Quality Must Be Preserved

- Output video should be **visually indistinguishable from the original**,  
    even when the video is **paused** (i.e., frame-by-frame analysis should still look clear)
    

---

### 3. ⚙️ Fast, Low-Cost Decoder

- **Decoding (playback)** should be **real-time**, fast, and require **low system resources**
    
- Especially important for:
    
    - Streaming services
        
    - DVD players
        
    - Mobile phones
        

> ✅ A slow **encoder** is acceptable because encoding can be done once, offline.

---

### 4. ⏯️ Support for VCR-like Controls

The compressed video must support interactive features like:

|Control|Description|
|---|---|
|**Pause**|Freeze the current frame|
|**Fast Forward**|Skip ahead quickly|
|**Reverse**|Play backward or skip backward|
