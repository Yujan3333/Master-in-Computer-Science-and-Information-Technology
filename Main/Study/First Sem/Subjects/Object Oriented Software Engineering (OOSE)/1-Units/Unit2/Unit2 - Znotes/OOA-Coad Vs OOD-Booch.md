|No.|Aspect|**OOA – Coad & Yourdon**|**OOD – Booch**|
|---|---|---|---|
|1|**Focus**|Focuses on **analyzing the problem domain** using object-oriented concepts.|Focuses on **designing the system architecture** and its components.|
|2|**Main Contribution**|Provides a **structured way to identify objects** and relationships during analysis.|Provides a **complete design methodology** including diagrams, notation, and guidelines.|
|3|**Approach Type**|Emphasizes **early phases** like requirements gathering and analysis.|Emphasizes **design phase**, including detailed class structures and object interactions.|
|4|**Modeling Aspects**|Uses **five layers**: Subject, Class & Object, Structure, Attributes, and Services.|Uses diagrams like **Class, Object, State, Interaction, Module, and Process diagrams**.|
|5|**Notation**|Uses **simple and intuitive notations** for beginners.|Uses **rich and detailed notations**, more suited for complex systems.|
|6|**Output**|Produces an **analysis model** with identified classes, relationships, and use cases.|Produces a **design model** including architecture, classes, subsystems, and interactions.|
|7|**Tool Support**|Mostly theoretical; tool support is limited.|Strong tool support (e.g., Rational Rose) — co-founded by Booch himself.|
|8|**Language Orientation**|Language-independent analysis method.|Heavily influenced by **C++** development.|
