
# ✅ **Image Preprocessing Techniques**

## 🔹 Introduction

Image preprocessing is the process of **improving the quality of raw satellite images before analysis and interpretation**.
It removes errors and distortions so that the image becomes more accurate and reliable.

* Geometric correction removes **spatial distortions** caused by sensor movement, Earth rotation, and terrain variation.
* It aligns the image with real-world coordinates using map projections.

---

## 🔹 3. Atmospheric Correction

* Atmospheric correction removes the effects of **haze, fog, dust, and scattering** caused by the atmosphere.
* It improves the true reflectance values of objects.

---

## 🔹 4. Image Registration

* Image registration aligns **multiple images of the same area** taken at different times or by different sensors.
* It ensures that corresponding pixels represent the same ground location.

---

## 🔹 5. Noise Removal

* Noise removal techniques eliminate **random variations in pixel values**.
* This improves image clarity and quality.

# ✅ **Advantage of Geometric Correction**
- [Adv of Geometric Correction](../../1-Units/RGIS-md-coll/Adv%20of%20Geometric%20Correction.md)

Geometric correction is very important before further analysis because:

* It provides **accurate geographic positioning** of features on the Earth’s surface.
* It allows the image to be **integrated with GIS data layers** such as maps, roads, and boundaries.
* It enables **correct measurement of distance, area, and location**.
* It ensures that features are in their **true spatial position**, reducing interpretation errors.
* It helps in **change detection and comparison** of images over time.

---

# ✅ **Conclusion**

Image preprocessing techniques improve the quality and accuracy of satellite images. Among them, geometric correction is especially important because it ensures that the image is properly aligned with real-world coordinates, making further analysis and interpretation more reliable.
