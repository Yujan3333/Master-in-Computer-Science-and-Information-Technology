# 1. Differentiate between Manual and Digital Interpretation of Remote Sensing Data.

| --------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |

| Suitable for small areas and complex scenes.                          | Suitable for large datasets and automated processing. |
| Lower processing speed.                                               | Higher processing speed.                              |

---

## Texture as an Element of Image Interpretation

**Texture** refers to the frequency of tonal variation or the surface appearance of an image.

* **Urban areas:** Usually have a **rough or coarse texture** because of buildings, roads, and other man-made structures.
* **Forested areas:** Usually have a **medium to coarse but more natural and irregular texture** due to trees and vegetation.

Thus, texture helps distinguish different land-cover types by analyzing the surface pattern.

# 2. Define Thermal Remote Sensing. Describe how thermal sensors work and discuss their applications.

## Definition

**Thermal remote sensing** is the process of detecting and measuring the **thermal infrared radiation emitted** by objects to determine their surface temperature.

---

## Working of Thermal Sensors

1. Every object with a temperature above absolute zero emits thermal infrared radiation.
2. Thermal sensors detect this emitted radiation.
3. The detected energy is converted into electrical signals.
4. The signals are processed to create thermal images, where different temperatures appear as different brightness levels or colors.

---

## Applications

* Forest fire detection
* Volcano monitoring
* Urban heat island studies
* Water temperature monitoring
* Environmental pollution monitoring
* Drought assessment

# 4. Why is early morning often preferred for aerial remote sensing missions? Discuss the significance of sensor resolutions.

## Why Early Morning is Preferred

Early morning is preferred because:

* The atmosphere is usually clearer with less haze and dust.
* Lighting conditions are more stable.
* Air turbulence is lower, resulting in sharper images.
* Cooler temperatures improve sensor performance, especially thermal sensors.
* Weather conditions are generally more stable.

---

## Significance of Sensor Resolutions

Sensor resolution determines the quality and usefulness of remote sensing data.

### Types of Resolution

* **Spatial Resolution:** Ability to distinguish small objects on the ground.
* **Spectral Resolution:** Ability to distinguish different wavelengths.
* **Radiometric Resolution:** Ability to detect small differences in energy or brightness.
* **Temporal Resolution:** Frequency with which the same area is revisited.

### Importance

* Improves image quality.
* Increases mapping accuracy.
* Enhances object identification.
* Supports better classification and analysis.

# 5. What are the functions of filters in remote sensing camera systems?

Filters are transparent optical devices placed in front of the camera lens to selectively transmit certain wavelengths while blocking others.

## Functions

* Reduce atmospheric haze.
* Improve image contrast.
* Enhance specific features such as vegetation or water.
* Improve image clarity.
* Reduce unwanted light.
* Protect the camera lens.

# 6. An image shows a measured road length of $4\ \text{cm}$. On the ground, the road is $1.2\ \text{km}$. Calculate the image scale.

### Given

Image distance:

$$
4\ \text{cm}
$$

Ground distance:

$$
1.2\ \text{km}
$$

Convert ground distance into centimeters:

$$
1.2\ \text{km}=1200\ \text{m}=120000\ \text{cm}
$$

### Formula

$$
\text{Scale}=\frac{\text{Image Distance}}{\text{Ground Distance}}
$$

Substitute the values:

$$
\text{Scale}=\frac{4}{120000}
=\frac{1}{30000}
$$

### Final Answer

$$
\boxed{\text{Image Scale}=1:30,000}
$$

**Interpretation:** **1 cm** on the image represents **30,000 cm (300 m)** on the ground.
