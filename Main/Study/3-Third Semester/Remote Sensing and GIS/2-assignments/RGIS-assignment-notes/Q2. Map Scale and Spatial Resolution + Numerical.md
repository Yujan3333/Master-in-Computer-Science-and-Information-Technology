
# ✍️ **Q2. Map Scale and Spatial Resolution + Numerical**

---

# ✅ **1. Map Scale**

## 🔹 **Definition**

Map scale is the **ratio between distance on a map and the corresponding distance on the ground**.

It is usually expressed as:

* Representative Fraction (RF) → e.g., **1:25,000**

---

## 🔹 **Explanation**

A scale of **1:25,000** means:

* 1 unit on map = 25,000 units on ground

Example:

* 1 cm on map = 25,000 cm on ground

---

## 🔹 **Types of Scale**

* Large scale → more detail (e.g., 1:10,000)
* Small scale → less detail (e.g., 1:1,000,000)

---

# ✅ **2. Spatial Resolution**

## 🔹 **Definition**

Spatial resolution refers to the **smallest object that can be detected in an image or map**.

---

## 🔹 **Explanation**

* High resolution → more detail (small objects visible)
* Low resolution → less detail (only large objects visible)

Example:

* 1 m resolution → very detailed
* 30 m resolution → less detailed

---

# 🎯 **Importance in GIS Analysis**

* Map scale determines **level of detail and accuracy**
* Spatial resolution affects **quality of analysis and interpretation**
* High resolution + large scale → better decision making
* Low resolution + small scale → generalized results

---

# 🔢 **Numerical Problem**

## ✳️ **Given:**

Scale = **1 : 25,000**

---

## 🔹 **(i) Distance on ground for 1 mm on map**

### Step 1: Convert scale

$$1\text{ mm on map} = 25,000\text{ mm on ground}$$

### Step 2: Convert to meters

$$25,000\text{ mm} = 25\text{ meters}$$

---

### ✅ **Final Answer:**

**Distance = 25 meters**

---

## 🔹 **(ii) Distance on map for 50 km on ground**

### Step 1: Convert km to meters

$$50\text{ km} = 50,000\text{ m}$$

### Step 2: Convert meters to mm

$$50,000\text{ m} = 50,000,000\text{ mm}$$

---

### Step 3: Apply scale

$$\text{Map distance} = \frac{50,000,000}{25,000}$$

$$= 2000\text{ mm}$$

---

### Step 4: Convert to cm

$$2000\text{ mm} = 200\text{ cm} = 2\text{ m}$$

---

### ✅ **Final Answer:**

**Distance on map = 2000 mm (or 200 cm or 2 m)**

---

# ✅ **Conclusion**

Map scale defines the relationship between map and ground distance, while spatial resolution determines the level of detail. Both are essential for accurate GIS analysis and interpretation.
