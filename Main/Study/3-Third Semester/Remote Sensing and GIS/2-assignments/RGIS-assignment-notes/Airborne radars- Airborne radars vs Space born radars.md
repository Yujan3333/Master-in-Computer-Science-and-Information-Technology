# Airborne Radars

Airborne radars are radar systems mounted on aircraft such as airplanes, helicopters, drones, or UAVs. They transmit microwave signals toward the Earth’s surface or targets and receive the reflected echoes to obtain information about terrain, objects, weather, or moving targets.

They are widely used in:

* Remote sensing
* Military surveillance
* Weather monitoring
* Mapping
* Navigation
* Disaster monitoring

## Characteristics of Airborne Radar

* Installed on moving aircraft
* Operates at lower altitude compared to satellites
* Provides high spatial resolution
* Covers smaller area at a time
* Easier maintenance and upgrading
* Flexible mission scheduling

Examples:

* Side Looking Airborne Radar (SLAR)
* Airborne Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR)

# Difference Between Airborne and Space-borne Radars

| Feature             | Airborne Radar                            | Space-borne Radar                                      |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Platform            | Mounted on aircraft or UAV                | Mounted on satellites                                  |
| Altitude            | Low altitude (few km)                     | Very high altitude (hundreds of km)                    |
| Coverage Area       | Small to medium                           | Very large/global coverage                             |
| Spatial Resolution  | Generally higher                          | Comparatively lower                                    |
| Revisit Time        | Flexible and frequent                     | Depends on satellite orbit                             |
| Cost                | Lower operational cost                    | Very expensive                                         |
| Data Collection     | Can be scheduled anytime                  | Limited by orbital path                                |
| Atmospheric Effects | Less atmospheric interference             | More atmospheric/path effects                          |
| Maintenance         | Easier to repair and upgrade              | Difficult after launch                                 |
| Applications        | Local mapping, military, disaster surveys | Global monitoring, climate studies, large-area mapping |

# Space-borne Radar

Space-borne radars are radar systems mounted on satellites orbiting the Earth. They are mainly used for:

* Earth observation
* Weather forecasting
* Ocean monitoring
* Environmental monitoring
* Large-scale remote sensing

Examples:

* Satellite SAR systems
* Earth observation satellites

---

# Key Point

* **Airborne radar** gives **better detail over smaller areas**.
* **Space-borne radar** provides **large-scale continuous Earth coverage**.
