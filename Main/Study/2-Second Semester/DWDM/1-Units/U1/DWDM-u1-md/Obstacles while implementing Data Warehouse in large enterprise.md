### Obstacles in Implementing a Data Warehouse in a Large Enterprise

#### 1. **Data Integration Problems**

   * Data comes from multiple heterogeneous sources.
   * Different formats, schemas, and DBMS make integration difficult.

#### 2. **Poor Data Quality**

   * Presence of missing, duplicate, inconsistent, and noisy data.
   * Requires extensive data cleaning and preprocessing.

#### 3. **High Cost**

   * High cost of hardware, software, tools, and skilled manpower.
   * Maintenance and upgrade costs are also significant.

#### 4. **Time-Consuming Development**

   * Data warehouse projects take a long time to design and implement.
   * Benefits are not immediate.

#### 5. **Scalability Issues**

   * Difficult to handle continuously growing data volume.
   * Performance may degrade with large data sizes.

#### 6. **Complex Design**

   * Designing schemas (star, snowflake), ETL processes, and metadata is complex.
   * Requires expert technical knowledge.

#### 7. **Changing Business Requirements**

   * Business rules and reporting needs change frequently.
   * Leads to redesign and rework.

#### 8. **ETL Challenges**

   * ETL is the most difficult and time-consuming part.
   * Errors in ETL result in incorrect analytical results.

#### 9. **User Acceptance and Training**

   * Users may resist switching from traditional systems.
   * Training is needed to use OLAP and reporting tools.

#### 10. **Security and Privacy Issues**

* Sensitive organizational data must be protected.
* Complex access control mechanisms are required.

---

### **Short Exam Answer:**
The major obstacles in implementing a data warehouse in a large enterprise include data integration difficulties, poor data quality, high cost, long development time, scalability problems, complex design, ETL challenges, changing business requirements, user resistance, and security concerns.
