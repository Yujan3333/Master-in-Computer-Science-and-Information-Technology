
# Open Distributed System Architecture — Multiple Viewpoints

A **distributed system** consists of many different hardware and software components that work together. To understand or design such systems, we look at them from different viewpoints:

---

### 1. **End User Viewpoint**

* Focus: How users **see and interact** with information
* Concern: Information content and accessibility from the **user’s perspective**

---

### 2. **Enterprise Viewpoint**

* Focus: **Business needs and policies**
* Concern: What the organization requires from the information system to meet user needs

---

### 3. **Computational Viewpoint**

* Focus: **Applications and software design**
* Concern: How distributed components and objects interact to support application logic
* Useful for programmers and system designers

---

### 4. **Information Viewpoint**

* Focus: **Information semantics and data management**
* Concern: Meaning, consistency, and structure of the data shared across the system

---

### 5. **Engineering Viewpoint**

* Focus: **System infrastructure and middleware**
* Concern: How system services support distributed applications (communication, coordination, naming, etc.)

---

### 6. **Technology Viewpoint**

* Focus: **Hardware and software technology**
* Concern: Physical components, network protocols, operating systems, and tools used

---

# Example: Telecommunication System

* End user: How a customer accesses phone or internet services
* Enterprise: Service provider's business rules and policies
* Computational: Application servers managing calls, messages, billing
* Information: Customer data, call logs, usage stats
* Engineering: Middleware that enables calls and messages
* Technology: Actual switches, routers, communication links
