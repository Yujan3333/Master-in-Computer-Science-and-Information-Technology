
# 🎓 Presentation Title

**Visual Image Information Extraction in Remote Sensing**

---

## 🟩 Slide 1: Introduction

* Remote Sensing is the science of collecting information without physical contact
* Images from satellites and aerial sensors are analyzed
* Two main approaches:

  * Human (visual interpretation)
  * Computer (digital processing)

---

## 🟩 Slide 2: Visual vs Computer Interpretation

### 👁️ Human Interpretation

* Based on experience and perception
* Uses visual cues like color, shape, texture

### 💻 Computer Interpretation

* Uses algorithms and machine learning
* Faster and handles large datasets

👉 Both methods are often combined for better accuracy

---

## 🟩 Slide 3: Visual Image Information Extraction by Human

* Manual analysis of satellite/aerial images
* Depends on:

  * Knowledge of terrain
  * Pattern recognition ability
* Common uses:

  * Land use classification
  * Urban planning
  * Disaster assessment

---

## 🟩 Slide 4: Visual Image Information Extraction by Computer

* Uses digital image processing techniques
* Methods include:

  * Supervised classification
  * Unsupervised classification
  * Object-based image analysis

### Advantages:

* High speed
* Consistency
* Handles big data

---

## 🟩 Slide 5: Remote Sensing Data Products

* Processed outputs from raw satellite data

### Types:

* **Imagery** (satellite images)
* **Thematic maps** (land use, vegetation)
* **Digital Elevation Models (DEM)**
* **Change detection maps**

---

## 🟩 Slide 6: Border or Marginal Information

* Information provided around the image/map edges

### Includes:

* Title
* Scale
* Legend
* Coordinate system
* Date of acquisition
* Sensor details

👉 Helps in proper interpretation of data

---

## 🟩 Slide 7: Image Interpretation

* Process of extracting meaningful information from images

### Steps:

1. Observation
2. Identification
3. Classification
4. Analysis

---

## 🟩 Slide 8: Elements of Visual Image Interpretation

Key elements used by humans:

### 1. Tone/Color

* Brightness or color variation
* Example: Water appears dark

### 2. Texture

* Smooth or rough appearance
* Example: Forest = rough texture

### 3. Shape

* Geometric or irregular
* Example: Buildings = rectangular

---

## 🟩 Slide 9: Elements (continued)

### 4. Size

* Helps identify objects
* Example: Road vs highway

### 5. Pattern

* Spatial arrangement
* Example: Agricultural fields

### 6. Shadow

* Gives height and shape clues

---

## 🟩 Slide 10: Elements (continued)

### 7. Site

* Location of object
* Example: Trees near rivers

### 8. Association

* Relationship with other objects
* Example: Schools near playgrounds

---

## 🟩 Slide 11: Applications

* Urban planning
* Agriculture monitoring
* Disaster management
* Environmental studies
* Military intelligence

---

## 🟩 Slide 12: Conclusion

* Visual interpretation is intuitive but subjective
* Computer methods are efficient but need training data
* Combining both gives the best results in RGIS
