- **HOOD** is about _organizing_ objects into a structured hierarchy.
- **FOCUS** -> Organization of systems through hierarchical decomposition using objects.

## Characteristics
- Top-down approach that breaks complex systems into simpler components. 
	- Break the system into hierarchies of objects/modules.

- Emphasizes parent-child relationships between objects.

- Hierarchical structure is the primary organizing principle.

- High-level objects delegate to lower-level objects.
	- Higher-level objects control and interact with lower-level objects.

## Strengths
**Well-suited** for *large, complex systems* where a clear architectural structure is crucial. The hierarchical approach aids in understanding and managing system components.

## Limitations
- Can be *rigid* due to the strict hierarchical structure, potentially limiting flexibility in object interactions. 

- May not be as well-suited for highly dynamic or distributed systems.
## Application
Initially developed for the *European Space Agency* and often used in domains like *aerospace, transportation, and nuclear plants*, with primary target languages like Ada, Fortran, and C.

- Embedded and real-time systems (e.g., aerospace, defense).
