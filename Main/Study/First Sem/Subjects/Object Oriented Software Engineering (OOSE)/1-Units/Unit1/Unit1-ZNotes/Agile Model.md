The **Agile Model** is a modern software development approach that delivers the product in **small, working parts** (called iterations or sprints), with **continuous user feedback** and **frequent improvements**.

It focuses on:

- **Flexibility**
    
- **Customer collaboration**
    
- **Quick delivery**
    
- **Responding to change**
    

---

### 🔄 How Agile Works:

- The project is broken into **small cycles** (called **sprints** or **iterations**)
    
- Each sprint lasts 1–4 weeks and includes:  
    🔹 Planning → 🔹 Design → 🔹 Coding → 🔹 Testing → 🔹 Review
    
- After each sprint, a **working version** of the product is delivered
    
- Based on feedback, the team **improves or adds new features** in the next sprint
    

---

### 🧠 Example:

You're building a chat app.  
In Sprint 1: You create login and send messages.  
In Sprint 2: Add group chat and emojis.  
In Sprint 3: Add voice messages — and so on.

---

### ✅ Advantages:

- High **customer satisfaction**
    
- **Fast delivery** of working software
    
- **Flexible** to requirement changes
    
- **Better teamwork and communication**
    

---

### ❌ Disadvantages:

- Hard to estimate **cost and time**
    
- Needs **experienced developers**
    
- May lose focus without clear planning
    
- Not ideal for **very small teams or short projects**
    

---

### 📌 Summary Line:

> The Agile Model develops software **incrementally**, with a focus on **customer collaboration**, **frequent delivery**, and the ability to **adapt to change quickly**.
