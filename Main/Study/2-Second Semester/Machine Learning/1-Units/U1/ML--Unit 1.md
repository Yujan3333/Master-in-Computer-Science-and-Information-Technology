| Type     | Order? | Equal Gap? | True Zero? | Example           | Trick                 |
| -------- | ------ | ---------- | ---------- | ----------------- | --------------------- |
| Nominal  | ❌      | ❌          | ❌          | Color, Gender     | *Name only*           |
| Ordinal  | ✅      | ❌          | ❌          | Rank, Grade       | *Order only*          |
| Interval | ✅      | ✅          | ❌          | Temperature, Year | *Equal gaps, no zero* |
